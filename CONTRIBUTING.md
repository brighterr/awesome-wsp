# Contributing

Thank you for helping curate Awesome Weight-Space Programming.

## What belongs

- Direct reading, construction, or editing of neural network weights by an LLM or agent.
- Compilers, benchmarks, replications, and tools that make these experiments inspectable.
- Selected foundational papers on program compilation, learned weight generation, weight-space representations, or targeted editing, with a clear connection to the topic.

General AI news, empty repositories, promotional landing pages without technical artifacts, and generic fine-tuning tutorials are not sufficient on their own.

## Add a resource

1. Search the existing list for duplicates, including earlier titles and versions.
2. Link the primary paper, author article, or maintained project. Prefer the official implementation.
3. Use the appropriate category. Do not label related foundations as direct LLM weight-space programming.
4. Provide the title, authors, publication year and verified venue or status for papers. Label blogs, preprints, research previews, and community experiments explicitly.
5. Write one or two original sentences explaining the mechanism and why it is useful. Avoid unsupported performance or generality claims.
6. For experimental projects, state material access conditions and limitations. Distinguish fresh model runs from replaying saved outputs.
7. Check links and Markdown anchors. Keep descriptions concise and end sentences with periods.
8. Open a focused pull request. Explain what you checked; disclose if you are an author or maintainer of the linked work.

Within foundation sections, keep entries in chronological order. Group updates to an existing work with that entry rather than adding duplicates. Keep the English and Simplified Chinese READMEs aligned in structure, resource entries, links, and evidence notes. When adding or updating an entry, update both versions; if you need translation help, note it in your pull request.

## Entry format

```markdown
- **[Paper title](primary-url)** — Authors. **Venue Year / preprint / blog.** Original description of the method and its relevance. [Code](official-repository-url).
```

For community projects, use the existing table and include both scope and limitations. Do not include benchmark numbers without linking the exact protocol and results.

## Corrections and conduct

Evidence-backed corrections are welcome, including negative results and failed reproductions. Discuss claims respectfully, link supporting artifacts, and distinguish a methodological disagreement from a factual error.

By submitting original list text, you agree to contribute it under the repository's CC0 1.0 dedication. Do not copy copyrighted abstracts or relicense third-party code, weights, or data.
